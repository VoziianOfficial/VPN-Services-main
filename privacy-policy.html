YOUR PRIVACY

How We Handle Information

This Privacy Policy explains how OrbitLock Digital Privacy handles personal information associated with this website, contact requests and related service interactions.

Last Updated August 8, 2026
Version 1.0
Applies To Website & Service Interactions
01
INTRODUCTION

Privacy Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the categories of personal information that may be handled when you visit the OrbitLock website, submit a form, request a free trial, ask about a plan or contact us about advertising, partnerships or other business opportunities.

The policy is intended to help you understand what information may be involved, why it may be used and the choices available to you.

Privacy-focused design: OrbitLock is presented as a privacy service, so unnecessary personal-information collection should be avoided wherever practical.

Scope

This policy applies to information handled through this website and associated communications. Separate product systems, billing providers, app stores or third-party platforms may have additional privacy terms of their own.

02
DATA CATEGORIES

Information We May Collect

The information involved depends on how you interact with OrbitLock.

Contact Information

Your name and email address when you submit a website form or contact us.

Business Information

Company or organisation name, project goal, inquiry type or proposal details when relevant.

Service Preferences

Selected plan, primary device, main use case or preferred region submitted through a trial or plan inquiry.

Message Content

The information you choose to include in a contact, collaboration or support-related message.

Technical Information

Limited technical information that may be generated when a browser connects to a website or hosting environment.

Cookie Information

Information associated with cookies or local browser storage where these technologies are used.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, authentication secrets or other highly sensitive information through the general contact forms.
03
SOURCES

How Information Is Collected

Information may come from several interaction points.

  • Directly from you when you complete a contact, collaboration, free-trial or plan-inquiry form.
  • From communications you send in response to an existing inquiry.
  • From browser or hosting technologies that support basic website operation, security and delivery.
  • From cookies or browser storage when those technologies are enabled and used for the purposes described in the Cookie Policy.

OrbitLock should avoid requesting information that is not reasonably relevant to the interaction or service being provided.

04
PURPOSES

How We May Use Information

Personal information may be used for purposes connected with the interaction that caused it to be provided.

  • To receive, review and respond to your message.
  • To process free-trial or plan-related requests.
  • To discuss business VPN requirements, advertising, media or partnership proposals.
  • To provide requested service information or support.
  • To maintain website reliability, security and abuse prevention.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory or contractual obligations where required.
Information submitted for one purpose should not automatically be treated as permission for unrelated marketing or commercial communication.
05
CONNECTION PRIVACY

VPN Service Privacy

OrbitLock is designed around a privacy-focused VPN concept. Website inquiries and account-related information are different from the content of normal internet activity carried through a VPN connection.

Before production launch, any statements concerning logging practices, connection metadata, diagnostics, infrastructure monitoring or retention should match the actual technical implementation of the service.

Important: claims such as “no logs” should only be published when they accurately reflect the real production architecture, operational practices and applicable legal requirements.

Service Diagnostics

If diagnostic or technical information is needed for reliability, troubleshooting or abuse prevention, the production policy should clearly describe the categories involved and how long they are retained.

06
DISCLOSURE

When Information May Be Shared

Personal information may need to be handled by organisations that help operate the website or provide related services.

Depending on the final production setup, this may include categories such as:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email delivery or business communication providers.
  • Security, fraud-prevention or technical-support providers.
  • Payment or subscription providers where applicable.
  • Professional advisers or authorities when disclosure is legally required.

Any production version of this policy should be updated to accurately identify material service-provider categories actually used by OrbitLock.

07
STORAGE PERIODS

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to an inquiry, maintaining business records or meeting applicable obligations.

Information Typical Purpose Retention Approach
Contact Requests Responding to questions or support requests. Retained only as long as reasonably needed for the interaction and related records.
Commercial Proposals Evaluating advertising, partnerships or business opportunities. Retention should reflect the ongoing relevance of the commercial discussion.
Trial / Plan Requests Handling the requested service interaction. Retention should be limited to the period necessary for the request and legitimate business records.
Security Records Abuse prevention, reliability or security investigations. Retention should be proportionate to the security purpose.
08
SAFEGUARDS

Information Security

Reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards should be used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure of personal information.

No internet transmission, website, email system or storage environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through general-purpose contact forms.

The website contact form asks only for essential information and includes server-side validation, email validation, input-length controls, honeypot protection and header-injection checks.
09
GLOBAL SERVICES

International Processing

Websites, infrastructure providers and digital services may operate across more than one country. As a result, information may in some circumstances be processed outside the country where it was originally submitted.

The production version of this policy should accurately describe applicable international-transfer safeguards and service-provider locations where required.

10
CONTROL

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may be able to request information about personal data associated with you or ask for certain actions regarding that information.

  • Ask whether personal information associated with you is being handled.
  • Request correction of information that is inaccurate.
  • Request deletion where appropriate.
  • Raise a privacy question or concern.
  • Adjust browser cookie choices where relevant.

Some requests may require reasonable verification before they can be completed.

11
WEBSITE TECHNOLOGY

Cookies & Browser Storage

The website may use browser storage or cookies for limited site functionality. One example in this website build is local browser storage used to remember whether the legal notice has already been acknowledged for the current notice version.

More information about cookie categories and browser controls is available in the dedicated Cookie Policy.

The legal confirmation mechanism uses a configurable local-storage key and version. Changing the version causes the notice to be shown again.

Children's Privacy

OrbitLock's general website and VPN service are not designed as a child-directed experience.

The production service should establish and publish appropriate age requirements that reflect its actual account model, payment process, intended audience and applicable legal obligations.

13

Third-Party Services & Links

OrbitLock pages may link to external websites, platforms, app stores or other third-party services.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Their handling of information is not controlled by this Privacy Policy.
