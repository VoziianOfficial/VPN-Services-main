BROWSER TECHNOLOGIES

How Browser Storage Is Used

This Cookie Policy explains how OrbitLock Digital Privacy may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies in connection with this website.

Last Updated August 8, 2026
Version 1.0
Applies To OrbitLock Website
01
INTRODUCTION

Cookie Overview

Cookies and similar browser technologies can be used to remember information, support website functionality, understand website use or provide additional features.

This policy distinguishes between cookies themselves and other browser-storage technologies such as local storage.

The current OrbitLock website build uses local browser storage to remember whether the site's legal notice has been acknowledged for the configured notice version.

If analytics, advertising, personalisation or additional third-party technologies are introduced later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated accordingly.

02
DEFINITIONS

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website or related service may store through a web browser.

Depending on their purpose and configuration, cookies may be temporary for a browsing session or remain available for a longer period.

Session Cookie

Generally available only during a browser session and removed when that session ends.

Persistent Cookie

May remain available for a defined period after the browser is closed.

First-Party Cookie

Set in connection with the website domain being visited.

Third-Party Cookie

Associated with a different external service or domain used by the website.

03
SIMILAR TECHNOLOGY

Local Browser Storage

Local storage is a browser feature that allows a website to save a small amount of information locally on a device.

Unlike a traditional cookie, local-storage values are not normally attached automatically to every HTTP request made by the browser.

OrbitLock Legal Notice

This website uses a configurable local-storage value to remember whether you have acknowledged the legal notice for the current notice version.

Storage Item Technology Purpose Duration
orbitlock_legal_confirmation Local Storage Remembers acknowledgement of the site's legal notice for the configured notice version. Remains until browser storage is cleared, replaced or the configured legal version changes.
The configured legal version is 1.0 . When the website's legal version changes, the notice can be shown again even if a previous version was acknowledged.

Technology Categories

Website storage technologies are commonly grouped according to what they are intended to do.

Functional

Strictly Necessary

Technologies needed for essential website behaviour, security or a feature explicitly requested by the user.

Conditional

Preferences

Technologies that remember settings or choices so a website can provide a more consistent experience.

Not Defined

Analytics

Technologies that may be used to understand website usage and performance if analytics are introduced.

Not Defined

Advertising

Technologies that may support advertising measurement, audience selection or campaign functionality if these features are added.

05
CURRENT BUILD

Current Website Use

The current website implementation includes local browser storage for legal-notice acknowledgement.

The site's own JavaScript does not require a marketing or analytics cookie in order to provide the core navigation, search, sliders, tabs, forms or page animations built into the project.

If analytics, advertising platforms, embedded media, customer-support widgets or other tracking technologies are added later, the production Cookie Policy should be updated before those technologies are enabled.
06
CORE FUNCTIONALITY

Necessary Technologies

Some browser technologies may be required to provide functionality specifically requested by a visitor or to maintain basic website security and operation.

For example, remembering that the legal notice has already been acknowledged avoids showing the same notice after every page navigation.

Blocking or clearing necessary browser storage may cause certain preferences or acknowledgement states to be forgotten.
07
SAVED CHOICES

Preference Technologies

Preference technologies can remember choices such as interface settings, previously selected options or other non-essential website preferences.

If OrbitLock introduces additional stored preferences, the production website should document them accurately and provide appropriate controls where required.

08
MEASUREMENT

Analytics Technologies

Analytics technologies can help website operators understand matters such as page visits, navigation patterns, device categories and website performance.

If OrbitLock introduces analytics technologies, the implementation should be reflected in this policy and any consent controls required by applicable law.

Do not list a fictional analytics provider or cookie name before the real production analytics setup has been selected.
09
COMMERCIAL TECHNOLOGY

Advertising Technologies

Advertising technologies can be used for campaign measurement, frequency management, audience selection or other advertising-related purposes.

The existence of an Advertise & Collaborate section on the Contact page does not by itself mean that advertising-tracking cookies are being used.

If advertising or retargeting technologies are introduced, their providers, purposes and controls should be documented before deployment.

10
EXTERNAL RESOURCES

Third-Party Technologies

The website may load resources from external providers, such as font-delivery or content-delivery services.

Requests to third-party services can involve technical information required for internet communication, such as IP address, browser headers or request information.

Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

If additional third-party embeds or services are added, their actual behaviour should be reviewed rather than assuming they do or do not use cookies.
11
USER CONTROL

Consent & Choices

Where consent is legally required for a category of cookies or similar technology, that technology should not be enabled before the required choice has been obtained.

The fixed OrbitLock legal notice is designed to record acknowledgement of the site's legal notice. It should not be treated as a substitute for a separate cookie-consent mechanism if one becomes legally necessary for future analytics or advertising technologies.

Important distinction: acknowledging a legal notice is not automatically the same as consenting to optional tracking.
12
MANAGING STORAGE

Browser Controls

Most modern browsers provide controls for cookies, site data and local storage.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • View cookies and stored site data.
  • Delete individual site data.
  • Clear all browser storage.
  • Block certain cookie categories.
  • Restrict third-party cookies.

Clearing OrbitLock site storage may cause the legal notice to appear again because the previous acknowledgement can no longer be read from the browser.

13
RETENTION

Storage Duration

The lifetime of website storage depends on the technology and purpose involved.

The current legal-notice acknowledgement stored through local storage remains available until it is removed, replaced or no longer matches the configured legal version.

Category Retention Principle
Necessary Storage Keep only for as long as reasonably required for the related website function.
Preferences Keep for an appropriate period based on the preference being remembered.
Analytics Any future retention period should be documented based on the actual analytics provider and configuration.
Advertising Any future retention period should reflect the real advertising technology and applicable requirements.
