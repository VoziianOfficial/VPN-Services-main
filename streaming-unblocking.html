PRIVACY COMPARISON

Same Entertainment. Different Connection.

A VPN does not change the content you choose. It changes the way your supported connection travels between your device and the VPN service.

Without VPN

Direct Network Route

  • Your compatible device uses its normal network route.
  • No OrbitLock server region is selected.
  • OrbitLock connection safeguards are not active.
  • Streaming service terms and local rules still apply.
With OrbitLock

Private VPN Route

  • Supported traffic enters an encrypted VPN connection.
  • You can choose from configured OrbitLock server locations.
  • The same privacy workflow can remain available while travelling.
  • Legitimate platform access, account rules and applicable terms still apply.
